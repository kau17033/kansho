Method

All six items, or no verdict

Every verdict is calculated from these six items. If even one is missing, the calculation does not get to count as “profitable.”

  1. Selling price
  2. Product cost (what you pay to source it)
  3. Shipping
  4. Selling fees
  5. Returns reserve (an allowance for returned goods)
  6. Other variable costs

There are only three verdicts:

Candidate

The conditions are met — profit remains after all six items.

Hold

Inputs are missing, or the conditions are not yet met.

Reject

No profit remains once every cost is counted.

Every issued record is signed, so later alteration can be detected. Verification is designed to work without going through the issuer — verification that does not depend on the verifier.

Honest Limitations

What the signature does — and does not — guarantee

What Outlire guarantees is the integrity of the records it issues. The signature makes it possible to detect whether a record has been altered after issuance. It does not guarantee future sales or profit.

A verdict is evidence that a calculation was run — “under this calculation, under these conditions, this product passed, or did not pass.” Outlire does not guarantee profit, and does not sell on your behalf.

Outlire is a newly launched service and has completed zero engagements so far. Please judge it on the disclosed method and worked examples — not on a track record it does not yet have.
